# Home plans row

`.door-plans` is the anonymous home's compact list of plans (`src/web/plans_row.ts`): rows, no box: name, muted price ("Free", "$19 a month", or "Paid" without a provider), an optional plain "Pay for <plan>" link, and one muted line of what the plan is; one hairline where the list ends. It sits in `#yours`'s single `.stack`, so its spacing is the stack's gap; the section's one large button follows it, and self-hosting is a sentence with a link, never a second button.
