# Display

A repository's display, `/:owner/:name/display` (`src/web/pages/display.ts`): the builds its `display.json` holds, as a dense table (`.table`, `.table-scroll`) under the repo header, as whoever looks may read them. It adds no component class. Covers, the player, editing the title waits for `pin_demos.ts` and the Arcade host.

Writers also get a form of `.field` fieldset, `.check` rows, a `btn` and a `.hint` status line: no new component class.
