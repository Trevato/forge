# Resilience

`/resilience` (`src/web/pages/resilience.ts`) is a forge admin's: the backups line, the nightly jobs, a tally, and one
`row()` per protection in THREATS.md inside a `card` `.list`, its standing a `badge` word in the trail.

## At 390 px (#9328)

A protection is a whole sentence and its cited test a path, so inside `[data-resilience]` (an attribute, not a class)
the row's title and meta wrap and long words break anywhere, in place of the list's one-line ellipsis; the row aligns
to its top so the badge sits by the first line. No fixed width; the page does not scroll sideways. No new class. The
owner's phone look of `/resilience` is taken on every preview.
