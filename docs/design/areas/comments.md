# Comments

A comment on an issue or pull, and the form that writes one. Both are a `card()`; these classes give it its comment look.

- `.comment-row` — a thread comment as a row of the timeline, no box: `.comment-head` (byline and time) then the body as `.prose`, spaced by padding under its `.tl-item`, no hairline of its own (the timeline line is the only rule). Written by `commentCard()` in `src/web/comments.ts` (issue page, pull page, browse); styled in `src/web/style/components.ts`.
- `.comment-head` — the one line of byline, verb and time at the top of a `.comment-row`.
- `.comment` — an inline review comment as a card: its `.card-head` (byline and time) on the muted ground, the body under it. Written only by `reviewRows()` in `src/web/diff-view.ts`; styled in `src/web/style/components.ts`, and spaced inside a review thread by `.dc-review .comment` in `src/web/style/content.ts`.
- `.comment-body` — a comment's rendered markdown, always beside `.prose`: padded inside a `.comment` card, unpadded in a `.comment-row` (written by `commentCard()` in `src/web/comments.ts`); `reviewRows()` in `src/web/diff-view.ts` and `reviewEntry()` in `src/web/studio/change.ts`; styled in `src/web/style/components.ts` (tighter in a timeline event's card).
- `.comment-form` — the form that writes a comment: a textarea and its `.form-actions`. On a thread (`composer()`, also the reply box on a studio change) it is plain lines under the timeline, no `card`, spaced like the home composer; the floating tell box (`tell_here.ts`) passes `boxed` and so adds `card`. A review reply (`reviewRows()`) and the studio box (`studioApp()`) write their own `card comment-form`. Written by `composer()` and `editForm()` in `src/web/pages/thread.ts`, `reviewRows()` in `src/web/diff-view.ts` (a reply in a review thread) and `studioApp()` in `src/web/studio/app.ts` (the "Tell the crew" box); styled in `src/web/style/components.ts`, with its narrow-screen action line and its review-thread margin in `src/web/style/content.ts`.
- `.tl-earlier` — the line above a long thread's newest items, `Show N earlier items`, a plain link to `?all=1`. Written by `conversation()` in `src/web/pages/thread.ts`; styled in `src/web/style/components.ts` beside `.tl-item`, indented to the timeline's item column.
