# The Plan row in a forge's settings (#11141)

A forge's admins see one `status-rows` `dl` row (`Plan`) at the top of `/settings`: the plan's name and size, the crew credit used this month, and the status (`Active`, or in grace `Payment due — paused on <day> · Pay`, #11394). With a billing portal set and a Stripe payer, **Manage billing** links to the provider's customer-portal sign-in (the buyer signs in with the email they paid with); **Change size** links to the host's `/pricing`. A forge with no notice (never told, or a host that is not its parent) shows no row.

- **Grace (#11394):** while a payment is late the row says `Payment due — paused on <day>` (the day grace ends, UTC) followed by one **Pay** link: the provider's portal when one is set, else the host's `/pricing`. Pay takes the place of Manage billing, and when Pay already goes to `/pricing`, Change size is dropped so the row never offers two links to one place. Home and board carry no notice.
- **Operator:** on the host's `/forges` (operator only) a forge in grace shows `grace until <YYYY-MM-DD>` in its row (`graceWords` in `src/spawn/clients.ts`); the terminal's forges screen says the same.
- **Host:** Settings → Billing → *Billing portal* sets `billing.portal_url` (https only, empty clears). Changing it tells every forge sent from here.
- **How a forge learns its plan:** the host writes a notice (`src/billing/notice.ts`) into that one forge's own `plan.json` (never `parent.json`) when the forge is made, a gift is redeemed, a plan is granted, or Stripe changes the subscription. The forge reads only its own file and calls nothing on the host, so it cannot read another forge's plan; the notice has no forge name, customer or subscription id.
- **Terminal:** the palette entry *Plan* (`src/tui/screens/planrow.ts`, any admin) shows the same plan line (`planLine`) and, for an admin who is a person, sets the billing portal address with Enter through `changePortalUrl`, as the page does.
- **Preview (#11549):** a preview is seeded in grace (`seedGrace` in `src/spawn/preview-seed.ts`, only when `FORGE_APP_ENV=preview`): its own `plan.json` says payment is late and one client forge is in grace, so the forge's looks of `/settings` (the Plan row) and `/forges` (`grace until <date>`), light and dark, desk and phone, are on every pull's looks page (`PREVIEW_OWNER_LOOKS`).
